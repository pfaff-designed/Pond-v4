✓Sharing and invitesA shared pond is one many people belong to, with roles (admins, moderators, members and guests by default; a pond can name its own). Moderators and admins can accept diffs on any page shared with them at edit, authors on their own, and the change log’s proposed view lists every proposed diff you can see across the pond. When someone leaves, an admin becomes the author of their pages, and their private pages are deleted. Threads stay private and never feed the pond’s memory. The pond owns its pages; each has an author. A new page is only its author’s until shared, and each person sees only what’s shared with them, except admins, who can open every page that isn’t private. Share a page with its nested pages as view, comment or edit, to a member, or to a guest from outside by email or by a link that can expire; a link only lets someone ask. Edits by anyone but the author arrive as diffs for the author, moderators and admins. A shared pond has its own memory, recorded only from shared pages and shown only to those who can open the source. Your own ponds stay yours; you can belong to several shared ponds.6n
✓Creating pondsSwitch from the rail header or ⌘K. A new pond is a short onboarding: name, glyph and theme, areas, sources, all skippable. Ponds are fully separate and each has its own theme. Rename, glyph, reorder, archive, export, delete (30 days). Same on mobile.6m
✓Layouts and screensLevel 4 is PondTemplate, the twelve premade templates. Level 5 is the screens in the shell, storyboards and examples. Every level of the library is built.5ah
✓View settingsEach view keeps its own filter, sort, group and visible fields, set from a small menu on the block.3a
✓Borrowed valuesA fixed set: show, count, sum, average, min, max, latest. One relation at a time; chain them to go further. No formula language.3a
✓Field typesCurrency is set per field. Changing a field’s type converts what fits, keeps what doesn’t as text in the same field, and logs it as a diff.3a
✓What Pond readsEverything in the current pond. Deep research reads the web, and so can any agent given the web tool. Every answer shows what it read.3a
✓Deletion and restoreOne trash per pond for pages, blocks, records and databases. 30 days. Restore puts it back where it was.3a
✓Conflicting diffsAccepting one diff marks any other that touches the same block as outdated. Pond offers to revise it against the new state. Two diffs proposed to one block before either is accepted are shown side by side: pick one, merge, or reject both. Comments are records in a comments database, anchored to a block in the margin; Pond answers only when asked. Presence is a name in the breadcrumb.6p
✓MemoryTwo layers, both per pond. Search over every page, record and thread, shown in the read chips. And a memory database of short notes that Pond records on its own, each citing its source, each in the change log, each yours to edit or delete. Deleted threads leave search at once. Sharing memory between ponds is for later.3a
✓ThreadsEach composer thread is a record in a threads database, kept until you delete it.3a
✓RemindersA property of a date field: it fires a set time before the date.3a
✓SectionsA heading and the blocks under it are stored as a real group, so it moves, nests and converts as one.3a
✓PondsA pond is a workspace, like one in VS Code. You can have several; each has its own root page and databases. Replaces “space”.3a
✓AreasA page with pages inside it. No technical meaning. Any page can carry a glyph and a colour; areas are where people usually set them.3a
✓TemplatesChanging a template changes no page made from it. A page keeps no tie to its template.6l
✓Change log and diffsThe change log is a database of every action. A diff is one record in it, with before and after. Pond proposes by adding a diff; you accept, reject or request changes.3a
✓External dataRead-only while connected. A source that breaks keeps its last copy. Disconnecting stops new data; the same database stays and its fields become editable.9a
✓MobileDoes what desktop does. Capture is the quickest part, not the only one.5v

✓Area coloursSage, mist and navy, set per area. Marks only: the glyph and its pages’ dots. Ink is the default.1a
