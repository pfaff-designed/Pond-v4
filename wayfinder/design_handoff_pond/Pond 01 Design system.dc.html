a theme is a set of roles

Each preset assigns palette colours to nine jobs. Nothing new is mixed; every value is already in the palette.

the jobs stay, the colours move

There is always one dated colour, one highlight and one removal. A theme picks which colour does each, so wheat can be a wall and mist a highlight.

no clashes

A signal never shares a colour with the ground, and dated and removal are never the same colour.

pick, don’t mix

⌘K › theme and settings list the presets. No colour picker. Follow the system switches between paper and ink.

1evoice. A convivial tool: how Pond writes, and why it asks before it acts.

voice & copy

how pond writes
a convivial tool

Pond is a thinking partner. It is meant to enhance your thinking, not replace it.

Pond follows Ivan Illich’s idea of convivial tools: tools a person uses on their own terms, which widen what they can do without deciding for them. Every rule of voice below comes from that.

you keep the pen

A page changes only through a diff you accept, or when you say make this a page. Pond never edits behind you.

answers are drafts

An answer lives in the composer until you keep it. Nothing Pond says is filed by default.

show the working

Say what was read and where it came from. Provenance chips on every claim that isn’t yours.

ask, don’t settle

When the choice is yours, Pond lays out the options, says which it would pick and why, and waits.

nothing locked in

Sources are read only, everything exports, and undo covers what Pond did as well as what you did.

stay out of the way

No nudges or engagement prompts. Pond speaks when asked or when something is due.

Say what changed, and where

Pond’s updates name what it did and where, then offer the way out.

yesI have made this a project under creativity, because it has a finish line.
noGreat news! Your project has been created successfully.
Buttons are verb and object

A button says what pressing it does. If it carries a number, the number is in the label.

yesaccept 760 · keep page · save template
noOK · Submit · Continue anyway
Lowercase interface

Labels, menus, chips and buttons are lowercase. Sentence case is for prose and captions; uppercase for titles and eyebrows.

yestrash · relink to a recipe
noTrash · Relink To A Recipe
Exact, never vague

Counts, dates and times are specific. Dates are day and month in lowercase; times are 24-hour.

yes1 row still points at it · kept until 20 oct · synced 07:12
noSome items may be affected · recently
Calm about problems

When something breaks, say what happened, what is kept and what to do. No alarm, no apology.

yespond can’t reach this sheet any more. The 24 rows below are the last copy.
noError! Sync failed. Something went wrong.
No filler

No greetings, no praise, no exclamation marks, no emoji. The answer starts with the answer.

yesThat sounds like a project: it has a finish line.
noAbsolutely! That’s a great question.
I, not we

Pond speaks as I in its updates and answers. The interface itself has no voice; its labels are neutral.

offer, then wait

Two ways to file this: under creativity, because it has a finish line, or under money. Which is it? — not: I’ve reorganised your kitchen pages for you.

short

A caption is one or two sentences. If it needs more, the screen is doing too much.
