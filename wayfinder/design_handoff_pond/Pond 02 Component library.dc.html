a task is a record

Tasks live in a tasks database. Board, timeline, table, calendar and list are views of it, so a change in one shows in all.

repeats

Ticking a repeating task makes the next record. Only the next one exists; later dates are projected and drawn outlined.

dates

A typed date is a suggestion until kept. Today is the one burnt-orange mark; late is said in words, never in red.

who changes it

Ticking, typing and dragging are your edits, applied at once with undo. A task from Pond or an agent arrives as a diff.
