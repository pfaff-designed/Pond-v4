pondA workspace, like a workspace in VS Code. Each pond is fully separate: its own root page, databases, sources, memory, threads, change log and theme. Sharing is the only way across. A shared pond is one that many people belong to; the pond owns its pages, each page has an author, and each person sees only the pages shared with them, except admins, who can open every page that isn’t private. Anything private belongs in your own pond. A new page is only its author’s until shared. Switch from the rail header or ⌘K.life; make noisestoryboard 6m · the rail header · 2r
shareAccess to a page and its nested pages, given to a person in the pond as view, comment or edit, from the row’s who can see. Someone from outside is invited to the pond first, as a guest. A shared pond has roles; admins, moderators, members and guests are the defaults, and a pond can name its own. Moderators, and admins, can accept diffs on any page shared with them at edit; authors always can on their own; in a shared pond the change log’s proposed view lists every proposed diff you can see, across pages. When someone leaves, an admin becomes the author of their pages; their private pages are deleted. Threads in a shared pond are private to the person who wrote them and never feed its memory. The databases the page’s views show come along, read-only unless they can edit. Edits by anyone but the author arrive as proposed diffs for the author, the pond’s moderators and admins. A shared pond keeps its own memory, recorded only from shared pages and shown only to those who can open the source. Pages shared with you as a guest are under “shared with me” in the switcher; nothing is copied into your ponds, and your own memory never records from them.launch plan, shared by Ana, can editstoryboard 6n · the rail switcher · 2r
pageThe only container. A page holds blocks, and pages nest inside pages. Each page has a kind, a label under its title (page, project, recipe, collection) that you can edit; it is set when the page is made and keeps no tie to a template. Life is the page at the top.recipes, and chickpeas, harissa under itscreen, in 04
areaA page with pages inside it. It has no technical meaning; it is how a person thinks about their pond. Any page can carry a glyph and a colour, and areas are where people usually set them.health, money, creativitymark · glyph · 2a
blockOne titled piece of a page. The / menu inserts them and the handle converts between them.the method checklistorganism · 2i, 2d
sectionA heading and the blocks under it, stored as one group. Its handle moves, nests or converts the whole section.method, and its stepsorganism · a heading plus its blocks
textProse on a page: a paragraph, a heading, a highlight, a page link. Set in the serif.“Needs more salt than you think.”atoms and molecules · 2a, 2h

sourceSomething outside Pond that fills a database: a sheet, a calendar, a bank feed, a health log, a CSV. Read-only while connected. Disconnect it and nothing new arrives; the same records become editable.transactions, from the bankmolecule · provenance · 2h

fieldOne typed value on a record: text, number, date, money, tag, checkbox, relation, link, image. Money carries a currency set on the field. Changing a field’s type converts what fits and keeps the rest as text in the same field, logged as a diff.time — 25matom · values · 2a
reminderA setting on a date field: fire a set time before the date. It is part of the field, not a separate thing.cabinet quote, 1 day beforemolecule · reminder · 2h
viewA block that shows a database: table, gallery, board, timeline, chart or big number. Each view keeps its own filter, sort, group and visible fields, set from a small menu on the block. It edits values, never fields.the recipes gallery, newest firstorganism · 2i, 2d
relationA field whose value is another record. Values read through it are borrowed, not copied: show, count, sum, average, min, max or latest. One relation at a time; chain them to go further.calories › recipe › kcalatom · relation · 2a
templateA saved stack of blocks with labels and placeholders. Twelve premade ones are generic shapes (page, notes, list, project, tracker, journal, collection, database, dashboard, meeting notes, reading list, habit); anything specific, like a recipe, is one you save yourself. Applying one leaves ordinary blocks with no tie back; changing a template changes no page made from it.project, tracker; weeknight recipe (yours)layout · level 4 · 2o
card layoutThe fields a gallery card shows, in order. Saved once, used by any gallery.image, title, time, tagmolecule · record card · 2h
diffOne record in the change log: what changed, before and after. When Pond wants to change something it adds a proposed diff. You accept it, reject it, or request changes, and Pond sends a revised one.electrician moved to w/c 6 octmolecule · diff line · 2h; in the composer · 2f
outdatedA proposed diff whose block changed after it was made, because another diff was accepted first. Pond offers to revise it against the new state.two diffs on the schedulemolecule · diff line, outdated state
change logA database in every pond holding one record for each action taken, by you or by Pond. Each record is a diff with its before and after state and its status: proposed, accepted, rejected, changes requested, outdated, withdrawn, undone or reverted.every edit, move, deletion and proposalorganism · table view of the log
undoSteps back through your own actions one at a time, back to when Pond was opened; the history ends when the tab or app closes. After that, revert any change from the change log. If the last action was on another page, undo opens that page first. Each undo is itself a record in the change log.⌘Z, ⌘Z, ⌘Zno part; a shortcut · 8e
freeform pageA page made of ink and images, for a pen, a finger, a drawing tablet or a mouse. It holds nothing else. Pond reads the ink you select, when you ask, and proposes what it read onto another page as a diff; the ink is never changed. Read ink is searchable.back room, sketchorganism · ink · 2s · storyboard 6s
inkThe strokes on a freeform page, and any image under them. Handwriting recognition is a source, from an outside service, that reads only the strokes you select; switched off, ink stays unread.three lines circled, read 11:20organism · ink · 2s
commentA note anchored to a block, in the page margin, by anyone the page is shared with at comment or edit. A record in the pond’s comments database. Replies stack on it; resolve closes it and keeps it. Pond joins only when someone writes @pond, and its reply is a draft.“too salesy for me”, on final copymolecule · comment card · 2t · storyboard 6p
moderatorA role in a shared pond. Moderators, like admins, can accept, reject or request changes on any page shared with them at edit, their own diffs included; authors always can on their own. The change log’s proposed view lists every diff you can review.Lee, moderator in studio collectivestoryboard 6n · 6p
zenA way of looking at any page with everything else taken away: the text and one pill (ask pond, editor, attach, type, ground, leave). Switched from ⌘K, ⌘. or by asking Pond. The editor, when on, suggests grammar and clarity as diffs.sunday, 21 sep, in zenorganism · zen pill · 2t · storyboard 6q
agentA role you ask Pond to take on in a pond, made by asking and accepted like a diff. A record in the agents database: its role, what it reads, its skills and its tools. Like Pond, it only proposes diffs; a change is made when you accept it. Pond itself is not an agent.health coach; financial advisorstoryboard 6t
skillA recipe an agent follows: when it runs (asked, from / or ⌘K, on a schedule, or when something happens), its steps, the tools it uses and what it makes. A record in the skills database, drafted by Pond when you describe it.plan the week, sundays 18:00storyboard 6t
toolSomething Pond and agents can use: an action on the pond (read, search, propose, create) or a connection outside it (web, email, sheets, handwriting). Switched on per pond; an agent uses only tools on for it.propose diffs, health › workoutsstoryboard 6t
trashOne per pond, for pages, blocks, records and databases. Anything deleted stays 30 days. Restore puts it back where it was.kitchen costs, deleted 22 sepblock · deleted state · 2i
threadOne conversation in the composer. Each thread is a record in the threads database, kept until you delete it. A thread, Pond’s or an agent’s, runs until it fills the model’s context; Pond warns before it does, and you compact it into a summary or clear it and start again. Only the person who wrote it can read it; in a shared pond admins can open every page that isn’t private, and never a thread.thread · life, 3 turnsorganism · the composer · 2f
memoryWhat Pond knows about you between threads, kept per pond. A database of short notes Pond records on its own, each citing the page or thread it came from and logged in the change log. Edit or delete any note and it is gone. Pond reads everything in the current pond when it answers, and deep research, or an agent with the web tool, adds the web. Every answer shows what it read. Deleted threads leave that search at once.“cooks for four on sundays”organism · table view of the memory database; read chips · 2f
one set of words

These are the words the product uses, in menus, settings and help. Atoms, molecules and organisms are build terms and stay in the component library.

a pond has one root page

Inside a pond every container is a page. Life is the root of the pond called life; personal, health and the rest are only pages with pages inside them.

conversion changes one thing

List to table changes the block and keeps its rows. Text to number changes a field type. Nothing around the change moves.

the record is the source of truth

Change a recipe and every gallery, table, chart and relation that shows it changes with it. Nothing holds a copy.

views edit values, not fields

Typing in a table cell writes to that record. Adding or renaming a field happens only on the database.

templates do not reach back

Changing a template changes no page made from it. A database changing its fields changes every view.
