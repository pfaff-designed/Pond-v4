6an edit is a proposal

Editing a page you are not the author of makes a proposed diff for its author, the pond’s moderators and admins. Your version shows only to you, marked pending, until one of them accepts, rejects or asks for changes.

author decidesonly the author’s edits apply straight away. A moderator’s edit to someone else’s page is still a diff.
