3one record

A record opens like a page: status, who proposed it, where it applies, the thread it came from and what Pond read. Before and after sit together.

this changerequest changes, revise and accept stay as linked records, in order.
read-onlyrecords can’t be edited. The log only grows.

4revert

Revert doesn’t rewrite history. It adds a new proposed diff that sets the block back, and links the two records.

a diffrevert goes through accept like any change.
anytimeany accepted record can be reverted. If the block has moved on, the revert arrives outdated and Pond offers a revision.

6deletions

Deleting a page is a record, with a restore button while the page is in the trash. The one date on the screen is the last day it can come back.

30 daysafter that, the page’s content leaves with the trash. The record keeps its name and when it went.
restoreputs it back where it was, and is logged.

Every memory note Pond records is logged, with the thread or page it came from. The memory view filters to them.
