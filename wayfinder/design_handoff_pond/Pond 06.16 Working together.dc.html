6review across the pond

In a shared pond, the change log’s proposed view is where review happens: every proposed diff on every page you can see, in one list, with the diff open beside it. Authors and moderators answer here or on the page.

moderatorcan accept, reject or request changes on any page shared with them at edit. Admins can too; authors always can on their own pages.
only yours to seethe list holds diffs on pages shared with you, nothing else.
namedeach accepted record says who accepted it.
