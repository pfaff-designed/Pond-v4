1into zen

Zen is a mode, switched from the command palette: ⌘K, type zen. It is the same page, not a copy or a separate editor. A new journal entry can be set to open straight into zen.

two ways inthe command palette, or asking Pond. Nowhere else.

4editor on

The editor button, a pen, asks Pond to read along for grammar, spelling and clarity. It marks what it would change with a dotted underline; each one opens as a small diff you accept or reject. Nothing changes until you do.

offby default. Switching it on is the only way Pond speaks unasked in zen.
suggestsgrammar, spelling, clarity. Never tone, never rewrites a paragraph.
loggedaccepted suggestions are in the change log as Pond’s, accepted by you.

9type and ground

Aa opens one small sheet: pick a style (text, h1 to h6), then its face and size; measure and ground apply to the whole page. The choices apply to zen only and are remembered per pond.

facesthe serif by default; the sans and mono are offered, not pushed.
groundspaper, wall or ink, all from the palette.
