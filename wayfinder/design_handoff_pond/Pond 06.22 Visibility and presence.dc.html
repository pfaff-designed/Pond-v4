1set who can see an area

Hovering a row shows ＋ and ⋯. ⋯ opens the row’s menu; who can see it is one item, and opens the four levels beside it. It covers the area and every page below it.

row menurename, move, who can see, delete. ＋ on the row makes a page inside. The same on every row, area or page.
fourprivate, shared, pond, web. One is picked.
belowevery page under clients inherits it.
new pagesa new page made at the pond root or under a private page starts private (only you). One made under a shared or pond-wide page takes its parent’s level, because a page below can be opened wider, never narrower.

2badding someone

Pressing add someone turns the row into a field. As you type, people in the pond who match are listed under it, closest first. Nobody is added until you pick one.

matchesfirst name, last name or email. The typed part is underlined.
picking↵ or a click adds them at view; change it in their field.
outsideonly people in the pond. Bringing someone in from outside happens once, in settings › people.
leavingesc or clicking away clears the field. Nothing is added.
