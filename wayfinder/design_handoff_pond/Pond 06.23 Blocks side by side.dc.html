blocks side by side
6xblocks side by side. A block can be ¼, ⅓, ½, ⅔, ¾ or full width, set from its handle. Blocks that fit on one line share a row; drag one beside another to make a row, or out to break it. Starts from 6g frame 3, a chart beside a table.

recipe
1a row of two, at rest

Details and method sit side by side, each half of the content column. The entry log and the cooked table below are full width. At rest nothing marks the row: no outlines, no gutters, only the same paper and shadow as any block.

widths¼, ⅓, ½, ⅔, ¾ or full of the page’s content column. A new block is full.
rowconsecutive blocks share a row when their widths add up to full or less. The next block that doesn’t fit starts a new row.
gap16px between blocks in a row, the same as between stacked blocks, so a page has one gap.
topblocks in a row line up at the top and keep their own height. The shorter one has paper below it.
yoursthe layout is the person’s. Pond never sets a width or makes a row. An accepted diff to a block’s content keeps its width and place, and a block Pond proposes arrives full, on its own line.

2set a width from the handle

Hovering the entry log shows its handle; clicking it opens the block menu. Width is one row in that menu, between turn into and the rest. Hovering ½ shows the log at ½ on the page before anything is kept.

whereone row of six in the handle menu. Nothing new on the block itself.
previewhovering a width resizes the block in place over 280ms, and blocks after it reflow the same way. The note under the row says it isn’t kept yet.
keepclick keeps it, with undo. Esc or leaving the menu puts it back.
currentthe width it has now is filled ink.
keys← → move along the row, ↵ keeps.

3drag a block beside another

The cooked table is held by its handle and carried up beside the log. A line in the gap shows where it will land, and the table already shows the width it will take.

linea 2px line in the gap, the height of the block it lands beside. A line under or over a row means a line of its own.
roomdropping beside a full block makes both ½. Dropping into a row with room left takes that room. If there is none, the row shares it equally: a third block into ½ + ½ makes ⅓ each.
old placeheld at 40% until the drop, so nothing moves under the pointer.
one changethe drop and any widths it set are one change with one undo.
motionlift 150ms; blocks make room and the drop settles over 280ms, on cubic-bezier(.22,.8,.2,1). Nothing overshoots. With reduced motion, blocks move without animating.

4an uneven row

Three blocks at ⅓ fill the first row. In the second, the log at ⅓ and the table at ½ leave a sixth over.

leftoverstays as paper at the row’s end. Nothing stretches to fill it.
moves upthe next block joins the row only if it fits the leftover. The narrowest block is ¼, so a sixth stays empty.
orderreading order runs left to right, then down. Moving a block beside another moves it in the page’s order too.
templatesnone of the twelve premade templates uses side by side; they stack, so they read at any width. A template you save keeps your widths and rows.

5break a row by dragging out

Dropping the table under the row puts it on a line of its own, full width. The log keeps ½, and the paper beside it stays empty until something fits or the log is set wider.

linea line across the content column, under or over a row. A block dropped there becomes full.
staysthe block left behind keeps the width you set. Pond doesn’t widen it.
undoone change, one undo.

6the same page on a phone

On a 390px phone every row stacks: the left block first, then the right, each full width. Tapping a block, here details, shows its handle in the block’s top corner. It opens the same menu, toward the free space, with 44px targets.

stacka row holds while each block in it gets at least 240px. Below that it stacks, in reading order.
narrowthe same rule on a wide screen: open the side panel or narrow the window, and a row that can’t give each block 240px stacks.
keptwidths and rows are kept, not shown. On a wider screen the row comes back as it was.
44pxthe handle, menu rows and width choices are 44px tall.
draglong-press lifts a block to move it up or down. Beside isn’t offered on a phone.
