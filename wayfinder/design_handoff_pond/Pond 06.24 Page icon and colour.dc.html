page icon and colour
6ypage icon and colour. Clicking a page’s or area’s mark in the rail opens a glyph picker anchored to it; areas also take a colour. Same family as the pond glyph picker in 6m.

workouts

tracker

1click a mark

Every mark in the rail is a button. Clicking the workouts dot opens the picker under it: search at the top, then automatic, recent glyphs and the library. Nothing is typed yet.

anchoredopens under the mark it changes, over the page. The row stays selected; the mark shows it is pressed.
before typingautomatic first, then the 8 glyphs last picked in this pond, then the library in its own order. The library scrolls; all 182 are there.
marksa page’s automatic mark is its kind dot. Choosing a glyph puts it in the dot’s place.
coloura page takes its area’s colour, always. It has no colour of its own, so the picker says where it comes from.

2search for a glyph

Typing narrows the library to glyphs whose name contains the text. The first match is highlighted, and its name shows in the foot.

names42 glyphs have names (flower, leaf, star). The other 140 have numbers and are found by number or by browsing.
keys← → ↑ ↓ move through the grid, ↵ picks, esc closes and keeps what was there.
pickpicking applies at once and closes the picker. It is your own edit: no diff, with undo.
noneno match says so, and points to numbers and the library.
→

3an area’s colour

Clicking the health mark opens the same picker, with a colour row under the glyphs. Each choice is drawn as the area’s own glyph in that colour: the colour only ever appears as a mark.

fourink, sage, mist, navy. Ink is the default and means no colour.
yoursarea colours are assigned by the person. Pond never picks one.
livea colour applies at once and the picker stays open, so glyph and colour can be set together. esc closes.
reachesthe area’s glyph and the kind dots and glyphs of every page inside it.

5back to automatic

Automatic is always the first row. Picking it puts the kind dot back on a page, or the title-derived glyph back on an area.

automatica page: its kind dot. An area: the glyph derived from its title, which follows a rename.
colourchoosing ink on an area puts its marks back to ink. Pages have nothing to reset; they follow their area.
undoeach pick is one change with one undo.
