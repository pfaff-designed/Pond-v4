Typing is grouped by pause, so one ⌘Z undoes a phrase, not a letter. Creating, moving or deleting a block or page is one step. An accepted diff is one step, however many blocks it touched.

one history

Page, rail and composer share one history. ⌘Z steps back the last thing you did, wherever you did it, and each line in the list names where it happened.

nothing is undone twice

Undoing a created page removes it and every block typed into it since, shown as a diff first if it held anything.

past the edge

Deleting a database is not in ⌘Z. It goes to the trash, as in 7a, and comes back from there.

fixture

A value in this ledger. It must match wherever it appears. Change it here first, then on every screen listed.

placeholder

Any other count or amount. Plausible, not binding, and free to change per screen.

the numbers tweak

Turn on “mark numbers” in Tweaks. Fixtures get an ink underline; placeholders get a dashed outline. Dates and clock times are skipped.
