what it does

Questions are asked in the composer and answered there, never on the page. An answer is a draft until you keep it, and keeping it always goes through a proposal card: name, place and kind. Deep research is the one mode that reads the web, with a plan and standards you agree before anything is read.

ask from anywhereThe composer floats over every page. Typing does not change the page underneath it.

2
the answer streams in the composerThe composer grows upward into a thread. The question sits in mono, the answer in the sans below it. Reading type is kept for the page. The page is untouched.
no bubblequestions are answered in the composer, never in the page. The page changes only through an accepted edit or make this a page.
stopesc stops the stream and keeps what has arrived.
3
the answer is temporaryNothing is saved yet. The answer stays in the composer thread until you keep it or move on.
unkeptclosing the composer or leaving the page keeps the answer in today’s thread. It is not filed anywhere.
ask againreplaces the answer in place. The earlier one stays in the thread.

5
the answer becomes a pageThis is the first time the answer touches a page. The prose moves across word for word; pond adds only a list of what it mentioned.
provenancethe made-from chip links back to the moment in the thread. It can be hidden, not removed.
undoavailable until you close Pond; after that, revert from the change log.
switch deep research on, agree the plan and its standards, watch it cross-check, see how it checked, and keep the answer as a page6r · storyboard 18
1
switch deep research onDeep research is a mode on the composer, next to send. It is off by default and on for one question at a time. It is slower than an answer on purpose: it reads widely, prefers primary sources and checks each claim.
one modethe one mode you switch on to read the web.
or ⌘Kdeep research from the palette.
2
the plan and its standardsBefore it reads anything, Pond shows the steps it would take and the standards it will hold them to. Change either, start, or ask it as a normal question instead.
standardsprimary sources first, two independent sources per claim, current guidance only, disagreement shown.
yoursnothing is read until you press start.
3
reading and cross-checkingEach step lists what it read, what it kept and what it dropped, and why. When sources disagree it says so and goes looking for the primary one. Stop at any point and keep what it found.
droppedsuperseded, secondary or repeated pages don’t count toward a claim.
pagenothing on the page changes while it works.
4
an answer that shows its limitsEvery claim carries its sources. What rests on one source is marked, and what it couldn’t settle is said plainly, with who could.
every claimcites at least one source. Nothing uncited.
not settlednamed, never guessed.
a draftuntil you make it a page or keep it in the thread.
5
how it checkedOne view of the whole method: each claim, its sources, and how well it’s supported. Then what was dropped, where sources conflicted and how that was resolved.
supporttwo independent, one source only, primary text, your pond, or not settled.
keptin the thread with the answer.
6
check a sourceA number opens the source: what kind it is, when it was published, which other source it agrees with, and the passage the claim rests on. Mark it not a good source and Pond redrafts without it.
passagethe exact words, in wheat.
your callyou judge the source, not Pond.
7
make this a pageMake this a page proposes a new page nested where you asked, with the answer as prose, the open question kept, and the sources as a block. Make page, change place or cancel.
sourcesstay attached, with dates.
nothing elsethe kitchen renovation page is unchanged.
8
back to your pondThe next question reads only your pond again. Nothing from the web goes into memory; a deep research page does, once you keep it, like any page.
off againdeep research never stays on by itself.
9
mobileThe same plan and standards, the same sources and open questions, on the phone.
same as desktopdeep research works on mobile too.
