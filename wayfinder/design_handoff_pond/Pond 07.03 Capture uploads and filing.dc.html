what it does

Capture from anywhere, on any device, including uploaded text, documents, audio and video. Pond files each thing on arrival as a proposed diff: with its home filled in when it is confident, and with up to three likely homes when it is not.

states and rules
uploadsnot drawn yet
whattext, documents, audio and video, from the composer, from capture on the phone, or dropped on a page.
arrives asa file block, the same block as attaching in zen (6q, frame 5). Open, move or delete it like any block.
filingan upload is a capture: Pond proposes its home as a diff, and asks when unsure.
readingPond reads an upload when it is filed or when you ask about it. What it read is shown as a source.
to drawupload from the composer and from the phone; audio and video playing in the file block; a large upload in progress.

1
into zenZen is a mode, switched from the command palette: ⌘K, type zen. It is the same page, not a copy or a separate editor. A new journal entry can be set to open straight into zen.
two ways inthe command palette, or asking Pond. Nowhere else.
escleaves, and puts everything back.
2
or tell pondAsking in the composer works too. Pond offers the switch rather than making it: one button, and the page opens in zen when you press it.
offeredPond never switches modes on its own.
leavingesc, or ⌘K › leave zen.
3
just the documentThe rail, the breadcrumb and the composer go. What’s left is the page’s own text, all of it at full ink, and one floating pill at the bottom.
ask pondthe Pond mark at the left of the pill opens the composer over it.
the pillask pond, editor, attach, type, ground, leave. Nothing else.
Pondis quiet. It proposes nothing while you’re in zen.
4
editor onThe editor button, a pen, asks Pond to read along for grammar, spelling and clarity. It marks what it would change with a dotted underline; each one opens as a small diff you accept or reject. Nothing changes until you do.
offby default. Switching it on is the only way Pond speaks unasked in zen.
suggestsgrammar, spelling, clarity. Never tone, never rewrites a paragraph.
loggedaccepted suggestions are in the change log as Pond’s, accepted by you.
5
attach a documentAttach in the pill, or drop a file on the page. It lands at the cursor as a file block, so it is part of the page and Pond can read it when you ask.
a blockthe same file block as anywhere else; open, move or delete it.
sourcesfiles from a connected source stay linked to it.
6
select to formatSelecting text brings up one floating bar: text style, bold, italic, link, list, then highlight, comment, ask pond and copy. It is the same bar on every page, in zen or not.
no chromeformatting lives on the selection, never in a toolbar at rest, anywhere in Pond.
7
highlightHighlight marks the passage in wheat, the only colour in zen. It is an edit to the page like any other, and ⌘Z takes it back.
wheathighlighted passages, and only those.
8
ask without leavingPress the Pond mark in the pill, or ask pond on a selection to send the passage with it. The composer opens over the pill; the answer stays there and the page underneath doesn’t change. Pond speaks only when asked.
a threadkept in threads, like any question. Close it and you’re back to the text.
changesif Pond suggests an edit, it comes as a diff, as anywhere else.
9
type and groundAa opens one small sheet: pick a style (text, h1 to h6), then its face and size; measure and ground apply to the whole page. The choices apply to zen only and are remembered per pond.
facesthe serif by default; the sans and mono are offered, not pushed.
groundspaper, wall or ink, all from the palette.
10
on the ink groundThe same page on ink. The pill and the selection bar flip with it; a highlight keeps its wheat and its ink text.
same rolesraw values don’t move; only roles reassign.
11
back to the pageEsc, or leave zen from ⌘K, brings the rail, breadcrumb and composer back, where you left them. The writing is already on the page: your own edits apply as you type, grouped by pause in the change log.
nothing to filethere is no draft to save and no step on the way out.
threadsquestions asked in zen are in threads, not on the page.
12
mobileThe same zen on the phone: the pill at the bottom with ask, editor and attach, and a selection menu with highlight, comment, ask pond and format.
same as desktopeverything in zen works on mobile.
