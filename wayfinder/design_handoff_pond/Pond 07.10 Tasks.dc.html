what it does

A task is a record in a tasks database; board, timeline, table, calendar and list are views of it. Tasks you type apply at once; tasks from Pond or an agent arrive as a diff. Ticking a repeating task makes the next record, and the tick and the new record are one change.

states and rules
where a project’s tasks live5ah · project template
1
a child databaseA project’s tasks live in a child database under the project page, shown in the rail as tasks inside kitchen renovation.
fieldsstatus, start, due, repeats, waits on, reminder. They belong to each task.
page detailsthe project page’s own status and due are separate from its tasks’.
viewsboard, timeline, table, calendar and list all read the child database.

2
enter, and it is a recordThe card lands in the lane and its record opens beside it. Every field is optional except the title.
recorda task is a record in the tasks database. the five views show it at once.
directtyping is your edit: applied at once, with undo.
3
repeatsThe repeat picker offers the patterns that fit the due date, then two choices: what the next date counts from, and when it ends.
next oneticking makes the next record. nothing is made ahead of time.
countedfrom the due date keeps a fixed day; from the day it’s done suits chores.
