the worn line in the floorboards

tuesday 15 sep — 4 min

Pulled up the last of the vinyl in the back room. Underneath, the boards are worn in a line from the door to the window, about a foot wide, pale where everything else has gone amber.

It is a record of the feet that made it, which is the thing Macfarlane is on about with paths, only indoors and on a much shorter timescale. Eighty years of someone going to look out of the window.

The plan was to level the floor. I am not going to level the floor.
