read only

Pond never writes back to a source. Values and fields change only at the source.

one tab, one database

Each sheet tab, calendar or account arrives as its own database under databases.

how often

Sheets every 15 minutes, calendars and bank feeds as they change, Apple Health from the phone. A CSV is a copy as of the day you drop it; drop a new file to replace it.

disconnect keeps a copy

Disconnecting turns the last copy into an ordinary database you can edit. Views and relations stay put.
