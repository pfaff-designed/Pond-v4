# Escalation mechanic: ephemeral → durable

## Question

One of the four session shapes is a general inquiry that turns into something worth recording. How does escalation work?

Questions to resolve:

- Who triggers escalation — user, AI, or both?
- What does the UI gesture look like? (a "save this" button? AI suggests it? command?)
- When escalating to a Document: does the conversation get summarized, or saved verbatim, or transformed into a structured form?
- When escalating to a Project: what gets pre-populated from the conversation?
- Can you escalate mid-conversation, or only at the end?
